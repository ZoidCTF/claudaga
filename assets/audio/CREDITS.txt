Claudaga - audio credits
========================

Everything here is CC0 (Creative Commons Zero, public domain). None of it
requires attribution; it is credited anyway because the people who made it
deserve it, and because a future reader should be able to check the licence
without having to go and find the source again.

No audio from the original Galaga is used, and none of this is a transcription
of it. The music was chosen to sit in the same chiptune idiom, nothing more -
Galaga's own music and sound are Namco's.

Sound effects
-------------
  shot_*.ogg      laserSmall_000/002/004
  efire_*.ogg     laserRetro_000/002/004
  boom_*.ogg      explosionCrunch_000/002/003
  bosshit_*.ogg   impactMetal_001/003
  die_*.ogg       lowFrequency_explosion_000/001
  beam_0.ogg      forceField_002

    from "Sci-fi Sounds" (v1.0) by Kenney - https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
    CC0. Unmodified; renamed only.

    boom_* is played at half volume. The files are untouched; the level is set
    in the sound table rather than baked into them, so the originals stay
    byte-identical to the pack.

  swoop_*.ogg     phaserDown1/2/3

    from "Digital Audio" by Kenney - https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio
    CC0. Unmodified; renamed only. Played at three eighths volume: one dive
    should be noticed rather than announced.

Music and jingles
-----------------
  title.mp3       "8Bit Title Screen" by Joth
                  https://opengameart.org/content/8bit-title-screen
                  CC0. Unmodified.

  bonus.wav       "Venus", from "NES Shooter Music (5 tracks, 3 jingles)"
  jingle_stage.wav    ("Warp Jingle")
  jingle_extra.wav    ("Intro Jingle")
  jingle_perfect.wav  ("Win Jingle")
                  by SketchyLogic
                  https://opengameart.org/content/nes-shooter-music-5-tracks-3-jingles
                  CC0. Downsampled from 44100 Hz to 22050 Hz mono; "Venus" is
                  additionally cut to 15 seconds - roughly the length of a
                  challenging stage - at the quietest point in a 13.5-17.0s
                  window, with a 0.25s fade over the cut.

                  The three jingles are additionally normalised to a peak of
                  0.80. They arrived peaking at 0.13 to 0.45 against effects
                  peaking at 0.90 - up to seventeen decibels down, and lost
                  under the shooting. SDL_mixer can only attenuate a sound, so
                  the only way up was in the file.
